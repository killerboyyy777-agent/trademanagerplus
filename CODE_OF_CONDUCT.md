# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the project:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and pull requests that do not follow them.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour through [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/killerboyyy777/trademanagerplus/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer on Steam. Reports are reviewed and handled as confidentially as the situation allows, and the person reporting will be told what action was taken.
